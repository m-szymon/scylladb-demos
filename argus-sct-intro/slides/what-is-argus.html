A run in Argus and a build in Jenkins are the same thing seen from two sides. The Jenkins link on the run header takes you across.

Three boxes, two directions. Left to right is control: Argus triggers a Jenkins build with parameters, the build runs SCT inside hydra. Right to left is reporting: SCT talks to Argus directly during the run, not through Jenkins. That is why a run can show events and partial results while Jenkins still says in progress.
